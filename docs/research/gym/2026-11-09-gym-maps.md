# Hallenkarte (gym map) – Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** `freemapper` makes a gym-map starter project for one sports hall (`--kind gym`, 1:200, range 1:100–
1:500). It contains:
- the hall's ALKIS outline as walls and floor;
- a fitted hall plan as an OOM template (`fit-plan`);
- court lines generated from the current rules (`add-court`);
- freemapper's own gym symbol set, aligned with the IOF draft ISInOM 6.6.

The mapper then draws the fixed map and the equipment in OOM. Courses and barrier tape belong to
freemapper-courseplan; freemapper-print adds a "Halle" profile.

**Architecture:**
- **Map kind:** `MapKind::Gym` (serde `"gym"`). Its pipeline downloads **only** the ALKIS buildings: no
  orthophoto, no height models, no land use, no OSM. `derive` has nothing to do.
- **Hall:** the ALKIS building(s) covering the map area (usually the hall's OSM way). The result is the
  `hall` part:
  - 1102 outer wall, with its outer edge on the outline;
  - 1203 floor.
- **Gym symbol set:** a complete OOM file written by freemapper, `assets/symbol-sets/freemapper-gym-2026_200.omap`.
  - `SymbolSet::gym(scale)` loads it and scales the ground-true (G) symbols by 200/scale.
  - Paper-fixed (P) sizes stay as they are.
- **`fit-plan`:** turns two tie points, or one known edge length, into a similarity transform. It writes a
  rotated world file next to a copy of the plan image and adds that copy as a `TemplateImage`.
- **`add-court`:** draws courts from per-sport rule modules into the `courts` part. Full-hall presets after
  DIN 18032-1 go into `templates/court-suggestions.omap` only.
- **Interfaces:** CLI (`fit-plan`, `add-court`, `--kind gym`), MCP (`fit_plan`, `add_court`,
  `list_hall_corners`) and the area selector (`Hallenkarte (1:200)`).

**Tech Stack:** no new crates. `image` and `quick-xml` are already in use. Least squares in closed form.

**Spec:** the user's decisions of 2026-11-08 (see the brief in the session). Verified sources (2026-11-08):
- `<Arbeitsverzeichnis>` (§n);
- the symbol proposal `<Arbeitsverzeichnis>` (§n).

## Rulings

- **Own symbol set, aligned with ISInOM 6.6, not copied.**
  - The ISInOM `.omap/.ocd` and OCAD's `Gym_100.ocd` carry no licence. freemapper draws every symbol
    (GPL-3.0-or-later).
  - ISInOM codes are used verbatim for what ISInOM defines. freemapper's extensions use **19xx**: no ISOM
    category 9 exists, and 1500/1600 are the IOF's likeliest next categories (GYM-SYMBOLS §1).
- **ISInOM sizes × 1.5 for map symbols, × 1.0 for course symbols.** ISInOM 3.1 allows enlargement, and the
  outer wall becomes 0.84 mm (≈ a real wall at 1:200). Course symbols are courseplan's; their size is
  open (Q1).
- **Two size classes.**
  - **P** (paper-fixed): line widths, signs and texts keep their mm at every scale.
  - **G** (ground-true): equipment preset footprints and dashed court-line patterns are metres and scale by
    200/S, clamped at 0.6 mm on paper.
- **The gym pipeline downloads ALKIS buildings only.**
  - The provider and CRS are still determined from the raster region, for the ALKIS service and the
    attribution.
  - Neighbouring buildings are not drawn: the map is the inside of the hall.
- **The outer wall's outer edge lies on the ALKIS outline.** The 1102 line is offset inwards by half its
  ground width (0.084 m at 1:200).
  - Without a covering ALKIS building, the map area is the outline, with a warning (and OSM attribution if
    the area came from OSM).
- **Tie points, not "control points".** "Control" is the orienteering Posten (glossary).
  - A similarity transform has no reflection and no shear. It is exact for two points and least squares
    for three or more, with the RMS reported.
  - A distorted scan is reported, not corrected.
- **Rotated world files only for fitted plans.** `GridSpec` keeps rejecting rotation, and `check_template`
  skips `templates/plan-*`. The OCAD background angle adds the plan rotation to the grivation.
- **Courts are building blocks, presets are suggestions.**
  - `add-court` writes into the map's `courts` part; objects are tagged with their court id and re-running
    the same id replaces only them.
  - Presets (single/double/triple) go into a template map and never into the map (ADR 0003 pattern).
- **DIN 18032-1:2014-11 sizes:** single 15 × 27, double **22 × 45** (22 × 44 from the brief is accepted as
  matching; GYM-REFERENCE §4), triple 27 × 45.
- **Court lines are neutral** (Black 50 %, 0.12 mm), with one code per sport. DIN colours are not used.

## Global Constraints

### Map kind and project
- `MapKind::Gym`, serde `"gym"`, CLI `--kind gym`, MCP `kind: "gym"`.
- Scale: default 200, allowed `100..=500`. Anything else is `ProjectError::GymScale { scale }`: "a gym map
  needs a scale between 1:100 and 1:500 (default 1:200)".
- Buffer: 5.0 m (`default_buffer_m`).
- `symbol_enlargement()` is 1.0, so north lines stay at the ISSprOM spacing of 30 mm (6 m at 1:200).
- `contour_interval_m()` becomes `Option<u32>`; it is `None` for Gym. The peripheral text then reads
  "Maßstab 1:200" without "Äquidistanz".
- Map parts in this order: `hall`, `courts`, `equipment` (empty), `peripheral information`. The candidates
  template is not written for Gym.

### Hall outline
- **Selection** from the stored ALKIS buildings (`underground == false`), with A = the map area:
  - H = {b : area(b ∩ A) ≥ 0.5 · area(b)} ∪ {argmax area(b ∩ A)}.
  - Outline = union(H).
  - Fall back to A when area(outline ∩ A) < 0.5 · area(A).
- 1102 per ring, offset into the solid by (0.84 mm × S / 1000) / 2 m. 1203 = the outline polygon, holes
  kept.
- Tags: `freemapper:source = alkis` (or `map-area`), `freemapper:source_id = <oid,…>`,
  `freemapper:currency`.

### `fit-plan`
- Command forms:
  - `freemapper fit-plan <DIR> <IMAGE> --at <PX>,<PY>=<TARGET> --at … [--at …]`
  - `freemapper fit-plan <DIR> <IMAGE> --edge <PX1>,<PY1>:<PX2>,<PY2> --length <M> [--onto <I>-<J>]`
  - `freemapper fit-plan <DIR> --corners`
- Pixel coordinates: origin at the top-left corner of the image, x right, y down, decimals allowed. Pixel
  centres are at i + 0.5.
- `<TARGET>` is `corner:<n>` or `<E>,<N>` in the project CRS.
  - Corners: the hall outline is simplified by Douglas–Peucker 0.20 m, and every vertex turning ≥ 20° is a
    corner.
  - Numbering starts at 1 with the corner of largest northing (ties: smallest easting) and runs clockwise.
- Transform: z = x − i·y, w = E + i·N, w = a·z + b with a = s·e^{iθ}.
  - Two pairs are exact. Three or more use least squares.
  - Warn when RMS > 0.10 m or any residual > 0.25 m ("the plan may be distorted").
- `--edge` mode: s = L / |p2 − p1|. The direction p1→p2 maps onto corner I→J, and p1 lands on corner I.
  - Default I-J: the longest outline edge, in clockwise order.
  - Warn when |L − |IJ|| > 2 % of |IJ|.
- Refuse:
  - points < 20 px apart or targets < 1.0 m apart;
  - a pixel size outside 0.001–0.5 m;
  - images other than PNG/JPEG by their magic bytes. PDF gets "PDF plans come later; export the page as
    PNG (300 dpi)".
  - images with a side > 16 384 px.
- Output:
  - `templates/plan-<n>.<png|jpg>` (n = 1, 2, …), a copy of the input.
  - A world file `.pgw`/`.jgw` with 10 decimals: A = s·cosθ, D = s·sinθ, B = s·sinθ, E = −s·cosθ,
    C/F = the transform of the pixel centre (0.5, 0.5).
  - The TemplateRef: Image, georeferenced, `crs_spec`, visible, opacity 0.6, behind the map.
  - A `[[plans]]` record in `freemapper.toml`: `relpath`, `source_name`, `sha256`, `method`
    (`points`|`edge`), `metres_per_pixel`, `rotation_deg` (clockwise), `rms_m`, `fitted` (date).
  - The existing map is kept; only its template list is refreshed.

### `add-court`
- Command forms:
  - `freemapper add-court <DIR> --sport handball|basketball|volleyball|badminton|futsal [--at hall|<E>,<N>|corner:<n>|plan:<PX>,<PY>] [--rotation <DEG>] [--from <P> --to <P>] [--size <L>x<W>] [--id <ID>] [--throw-off-circle] [--with-equipment]`
  - `freemapper add-court <DIR> --preset single|double|triple`
  - `freemapper add-court <DIR> --remove <ID>`
- Defaults: the centre and long-axis direction of the minimum-area rectangle of the hall outline.
  `--rotation` is the court's long axis in degrees clockwise from grid north.
- `--from/--to` are the midpoints of the two end lines (outer edge). Expected lengths: handball 40.00,
  basketball 28.10, volleyball 18.00, badminton 13.40, futsal L. Warn when the measured length is off by
  more than 2 %.
- **Rules editions** (tag `freemapper:rules`) and geometry (GYM-REFERENCE §3):
  - **IHF 2025-07-01:**
    - 40 × 20 outer; lines 0.05, goal line between the posts 0.08;
    - goal area: arcs r 6 centred at the rear inner post corners (±1.5 m on the rear edge of the goal
      line), plus a 3 m line at 6 m;
    - 9 m line concentric with it, 1956 dashed with 0.15/0.15;
    - 7 m line 1.00 m, front edge at 7 m; 4 m line 0.15 m;
    - substitution marks at 4.5 m from the centre line, 0.15 m inside and 0.15 m outside the side line;
    - optional throw-off circle Ø 4 m.
  - **FIBA OBR 2026:**
    - 28 × 15 inner edges; lines 0.05, lying outside the court for the boundary;
    - centre line +0.15 beyond the sidelines; centre circle and free-throw semicircles r 1.80 to the outer
      edge;
    - free-throw line: far edge 5.80, length 3.60; restricted area 4.90 wide;
    - 3-point line: side parts with their outer edge 0.90 from the sideline inner edge; arc r 6.75 to the
      outer edge around the point 1.575 from the endline inner edge;
    - no-charge semicircle r 1.30 to the outer edge, plus 0.375 lines ending 1.20 from the endline;
    - throw-in lines 0.15 outside, outer edge at 8.325.
    - `--size` 26–28 × 14–15 (DIN A.1).
  - **FIVB 2025–2028:**
    - 18 × 9, lines 0.05 inside the court;
    - attack line: rear edge 3.00 from the centre-line axis;
    - service marks 0.15 m long, 0.20 m behind the end line.
    - The attack-line extension (5 × 0.15, 0.20 apart) is optional (`--fivb-extension`).
  - **BWF Laws Law 1 / Diagram A:**
    - 13.40 × 6.10 outer; lines 0.04;
    - singles sideline: outer edge 0.46 m inside the doubles outer edge (0.04 line + 0.42 gap), so the
      singles court is 5.18 m wide;
    - short service line: near edge 1.98 m from the net;
    - doubles long service line: rear edge 0.76 m from the outer edge of the back boundary;
    - centre lines from the short service line to the back boundary.
  - **FIFA Futsal LotG 2025-26:**
    - default 40 × 20; `--size` 25–42 × 16–25 with L > W; lines 0.08, measured outside;
    - centre circle r 3 and centre mark r 0.06;
    - penalty area: arcs r 6 from the outside of each post, joined by 3.16;
    - penalty mark at 6 m, 10 m mark, marks 5 m either side of the 10 m mark, 5 m mark 0.40 long;
    - corner arcs r 0.25; corner marks 5 m from the arc, outside, 0.40 long with a 0.05 gap;
    - substitution marks 0.80 long (0.40 in, 0.40 out) at 5 m and 10 m from the halfway line.
- **Drawing rules:**
  - Each painted line is drawn as its centre line, offset by half its width from the rule's measuring edge.
  - Arcs are polylines with a deviation ≤ 0.01 m.
  - Codes: 1951–1955 by sport, 1956 for the 9 m line, 1957 for spots.
- **Tags:** `freemapper:source = court-rules`, `freemapper:sport`, `freemapper:court-id` (default
  `<sport>-<n>`), `freemapper:rules`.
- `--with-equipment` writes into the `equipment` part:
  - goals 1930 (handball, futsal);
  - baskets 1931, anchored at 1.575 from the endline inner edge (basketball);
  - posts 1934 at 0.50 outside the sidelines and a net 1933 (volleyball);
  - posts on the doubles sidelines and a net (badminton).
- **Safety:** the `.omap` is written atomically after a `<name>.omap.bak` copy. Only objects tagged with
  the given court id in `courts`/`equipment` are replaced or removed.
- **Presets → `templates/court-suggestions.omap`** (TemplateMap, opacity 0.7, in front). The hall's inner
  rectangle = the minimum-area rectangle minus 0.30 m per wall; warn when it differs from the preset by
  more than 1.5 m.
  - **single 15 × 27:** volleyball 18 × 9 and basketball 26 × 14, both longitudinal and centred; 3 badminton
    courts transverse, centres at −8.275, 0, +8.275 m along the long axis.
  - **double 22 × 45:** handball 40 × 20, basketball 28 × 15 and volleyball 18 × 9, all longitudinal and
    centred; 4 badminton courts transverse at −13.5, −4.5, +4.5, +13.5 m.
  - **triple 27 × 45:**
    - handball 40 × 20, basketball 28 × 15 and volleyball 18 × 9, longitudinal and centred;
    - per third (centres −15, 0, +15 m): volleyball 18 × 9 and basketball 26 × 14 transverse;
    - 2 badminton courts transverse at ±3.75 m from the third's centre.

## Review Focus

1. **World-file conventions:**
   - pixel corner vs. centre, y-down, the sign of θ in A/B/D/E;
   - OOM's `WorldFile → QTransform` (offset (A+B)/2, (D+E)/2) puts the plan where the fit put it;
   - the OCAD background angle includes the plan rotation, and unrotated rasters are unchanged.
2. **Court geometry against the rules:**
   - the measuring edge per federation (FIBA inner edge with lines outside, IHF rear edge, lines part of the
     area elsewhere) and the half-width offsets;
   - arc centres (IHF: rear inner post corner; futsal: outside of the post);
   - the concentric 9 m line, and the basket and board positions.
3. **Size classes:**
   - G footprints and patterns scale by 200/S with the 0.6 mm clamp; P widths never change;
   - the Schoolyard and Sprint symbol sets are byte-identical to before (191 symbols, etc.).
4. **Gym isolation:**
   - a gym `fetch` issues no orthophoto, DGM/DOM/nDSM, land-use or OSM request (recording fetcher);
   - hall selection and fallback follow the rule above;
   - Schoolyard and Sprint behave as before.
5. **User work is safe:**
   - `add-court` and `fit-plan` touch only their own objects and templates and keep `.omap.bak`;
   - `--remove` removes exactly one court id;
   - OCAD numbers: Gym gets 520.1 → 520.001 and the 19xx plain rule; the existing tables are unchanged.
