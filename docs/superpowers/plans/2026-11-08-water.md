# Water – Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Rivers, canals, ponds, harbours and fountain basins are drawn. ALKIS water parcels become ISSprOM 301 map
objects, and OSM water features become candidates. This applies to both map kinds, in OOM, OCAD and the PDF.

**Architecture:** A new `build/water.rs` sits next to `paved.rs` and `out_of_bounds.rs`.
- ALKIS: the water parts of `LandUse` are dissolved into one shape. That shape is clipped with `LandClip`
  (`out_of_bounds.rs:68`, `:91`) and becomes a 301.1 fill plus 301.6 bank lines.
- OSM: new rules in `assets/rules/osm-candidates.toml`. They go through the existing `Rules`/`osm_objects`
  (`candidates.rs:220`, `:261`). OSM water areas that already lie inside ALKIS water are dropped.
- Wiring: `starter_documents` (`project.rs:2650–2725`), next to `paved.map` and `paved.candidates`
  (`project.rs:2676`, `:2720`).
- The OCAD writer and the PDF renderer read the symbol set generically. Only the OCAD number table changes.

**Tech Stack:** no new crates (`geo` `BooleanOps` for union and clip).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-freemapper-design.md` §4.5 gets a "Water" block. Evidence (2026-11-08):
- symbols: `assets/symbol-sets/ISSprOM 2019_4000.omap:198–252`;
- OCAD numbers: decoded from OCAD's `ISSprOM_blank.ocd` (scratchpad `symres/pp/`);
- OSM tags: OOM's `OSM-ISSprOM 2019.crt:39–146` (`/Applications/Mapper.app/Contents/Resources/symbol sets/`);
- ALKIS water: stored `data/alkis-land-use.geojson` of 20 projects (BB, SN, ST, HH, NI), plus the NI and NRW fixtures
  (`Stehendes Gewässer`/`Teich`).

## Rulings

- **ALKIS water is reliable: it becomes 301 (uncrossable), never 302.** ALKIS has no depth. The parcel edge may
  include the banks, and the mapper fixes it from the orthophoto.
- **The fill and the bank line are separate symbols (301.1 + 301.6), not the combined 301.**
  - A river is split into many parcels (33 in one Hannover area).
  - The combined symbol would draw a black line at every parcel boundary and along the clip edge (= map edge).
- **The width rule replaces `bez`.** BB, SN and ST give no `bez`, so `Graben` cannot be told apart from `Fluss`
  there. A part with a mean width (2·area/perimeter) under 2 m (the ISSprOM 305 limit) is a 301 candidate.
- **No relations.** `read_extract` skips relations (`osm.rs:132`), so multipolygon rivers are not read; ALKIS covers
  them. Relation support stays a later option.
- **River and canal centre lines get no candidate.** Their area comes from ALKIS or `natural=water`, and a line would
  have the wrong symbol.
- **Marsh (307–310) is out of scope.** It is rare in sprint areas; `Moor` and `Sumpf` stay unmapped.
- **Bridges come only from OSM.** ALKIS-vereinfacht `GebaeudeBauwerk` has no bridges (fixtures: only `Gebäude`,
  `Bauteil` and `Sonstiges Bauwerk`). `man_made=bridge` areas become 501.5 candidates on the sprint map.
- **Schoolyard: water off the grounds stays inside the 520 candidates** (`out_of_bounds.rs:48`). Blue (priority 16)
  paints over olive (22).
- **A sea missing from ALKIS stays white.** `natural=coastline` is not read.

## Global Constraints

- **ALKIS → map (both kinds):**
  - `WATER_LAND_USES = ["Fließgewässer", "Stehendes Gewässer", "Hafenbecken", "Meer"]` (any `bez`).
  - Spellings are already normalised: LGB, the WFS states (`alkis_wfs.rs:28–121`), Hamburg (`lgv.rs:266–272`;
    `bez` "Fluß"), and Berlin (`berlin.rs:50–54`, which has no `AX_Meer`).
  - Steps:
    1. Every part with mean width ≥ `NARROW_WATER_M = 2.0` m is unioned.
    2. The union is clipped by `LandClip`; slivers under 1 mm on paper are dropped.
    3. Each piece becomes one **301.1** area, holes = islands.
    4. Each ring becomes **301.6** lines, with the segments on the clip rectangle removed (both ends within 0.01 m
       of the same edge). A ring without such segments stays closed.
  - Narrower parts become **301.1 + 301.6 candidates**, built the same way but not unioned with the rest.
  - Tags:
    - `freemapper:source=alkis-nutzung`;
    - `freemapper:source_id` = the sorted oids joined by `;`;
    - `freemapper:nutzart` = the distinct `nutzart / bez`, joined by `;`;
    - `freemapper:currency` = the newest `updated`.
- **OSM → candidates (rules in `osm-candidates.toml`, both kinds unless noted):**

  | `when` | draw | code |
  |---|---|---|
  | `natural=water`, `covered!=yes`, `tunnel!=*` | area | 301 |
  | `waterway=riverbank` | area | 301 |
  | `landuse=reservoir` | area | 301 |
  | `landuse=basin`, `basin!=retention\|infiltration\|detention` | area | 301 |
  | `leisure=swimming_pool`, `indoor!=yes`, `covered!=yes`, `location!=indoor\|underground\|roof` | area | 301 |
  | `amenity=fountain` | area | 301 |
  | `amenity=fountain` | point | 311 |
  | `man_made=water_well` | point | 311 |
  | `natural=spring` | point | 312 |
  | `amenity=drinking_water` (schoolyard only) | point | 313 |
  | `waterway=stream\|drain`, `intermittent!=yes`, `tunnel!=*` | line | 305 |
  | `waterway=stream\|drain\|ditch`, `tunnel!=*` | line | 306 |
  | `man_made=bridge` (sprint only) | area | 501.5 |

  The combined 301 is used for OSM areas because they are not clipped. A 301 OSM area with at least 50 % of its area
  inside the ALKIS water union is dropped (`OSM_WATER_COVERED = 0.5`).
- **Order:** OOM and the PDF paint by colour priority (`render/pdf.rs:196`):
  - bank line 301.6 black (2) on top;
  - then buildings 521.3 (9) and paved 501.5 (14);
  - then water 301.1 (16) and out of bounds 520 (22).

  So bridges and paved parcels cover water. No object order is needed.
- **OCAD (`ocd/symbols.rs:15`):** add `("301.1", 301_001)` and `("301.6", 301_002)`. The decimal rule would give
  301100 and 301600, which are absent in the OCAD set. 301, 305, 306, 311, 312 and 313 follow the rule
  (301000 … 313000, checked against `ISSprOM_blank.ocd`).
- **Schoolyard (1:1000, symbols at 125 %):** the same rules. The sliver limits scale with the denominator (1 mm =
  1 m); `NARROW_WATER_M` stays 2 m on the ground.

## Review Focus

1. **No black lines across water:** none at parcel boundaries, none along the map edge. Islands keep their bank
   line.
2. **ALKIS spellings per state** (Hamburg `Fluß`, missing `bez` in BB/SN/ST). Non-water land use is unchanged.
3. **Paint order:** bridges and paved parcels lie over water, and 520 lies under it. The bank line across a bridge
   is a known limit (mapper fix).
4. **OCAD numbers** 301001 and 301002, and the placeholders keep their type (area, line).
5. **No duplicates:** an OSM pond inside an ALKIS lake is dropped. The extract filter (`Rules::wants`) grows only by
   the water tags, and existing maps and candidates are unchanged apart from water.
