# Hesse Adapter – Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Maps in Hesse get orthophoto, terrain, nDSM, ALKIS buildings and land use and OSM candidates, in
EPSG:25832 like Saxony-Anhalt and Hamburg.

**Architecture:** The third zone-32 provider, HVBG Hesse (`Provider::Hvbg`, rasters per area).
- Orthophoto from the free WMS (pieces ≤ 3000 px), flight date by GetFeatureInfo.
- DGM1 and DOM1 1 km tiles read by HTTP range out of the Downloadcenter's municipal zips, mosaicked into
  `data/dgm1.tif` / `data/dom1.tif`:
  - tile → municipality and district from the DGM/DOM metadata xlsx (which also gives the date);
  - municipality → zip URL from the Downloadcenter REST listing, resolved before every download;
  - members through the zip-range reader shared with Hamburg.
- ALKIS from the AAA-suite WFS through the shared `alkis_wfs` reader (`ave:NutzungFlurstueck`).

**Tech Stack:** no new crates (xlsx is a zip of XML: `zip` + `quick-xml`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-freemapper-design.md` §4.1. Verified sources (2026-11-07/08):
`docs/regions/hessen-reference.md` (cited as §n). Live samples (untrusted data, for fixtures only):
`/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-henryprivat-Documents-gitrepos-freemapper/626dafd0-2cfb-4a8b-9864-ebf6d40c8bb0/scratchpad/hessen/`.

## Rulings (decided before the plan, from §13 item 2's open decision)

- **DOM1 must come from the download tiles** (§6.1: the WCS `dom1` is a different surface). The Downloadcenter
  path is therefore built, and DGM1 uses it too (identical to the WCS, 4× faster, dated per tile).
- **The orthophoto stays on the WMS** (same pixels as the tiles, §4.2). Its pieces are smaller (3000 px) and
  retried. Reading the DOP tiles from the Downloadcenter is left for later.
- The xlsx is the only tile → municipality index that also gives the date. Its URL is taken from the REST product
  description, never pinned.

## Global Constraints

- **Region:**
  - `Region::Hessen` (serde `"hessen"`), boundary `assets/regions/hessen.geojson` (from the scratchpad
    `probe/boundary/hessen_simplified25m.geojson`; 25833 with **negative eastings**).
  - CRS `Utm32`, provider `Provider::Hvbg` (serde `"hvbg"`, `per_area()` true), object ids `DEHE…`.
  - OSM extract `europe/germany/hessen-latest.osm.pbf` (348 MB), cached as `hessen_<date>.osm.pbf`.
- **Negative 25833 coordinates (§3):**
  - No 25833 `TileId` may be computed for Hesse.
  - `regions_for`/`raster_region`, the boundary loader and every area/estimate path must work with eastings < 0.
- **Orthophoto (§4.2):**
  - `https://www.gds-srv.hessen.de/cgi-bin/lika-services/ogc-free-images.ows`, layer `he_dop20_rgb`,
    `CRS=EPSG:25832`, JPEG.
  - `WmsService` gains `max_px: u32`: 3000 here, 4000 for GeoSN, LVermGeo and LGV. `WMS_MAX_PX` goes.
  - A GetMap answered with XML is an error (§4.2: HTTP 200 `text/xml`).
- **Flight date (§4.2):** GetFeatureInfo on `he_dop_rgb_info` at the centre (`INFO_FORMAT=text/plain`),
  `bildflug = 'DD-MM-YYYY'` → `DD.MM.YYYY`. "Search returned no results" → `None`.
- **DGM1/DOM1 (§2.2, §5, §6.1, §7):**
  - REST `https://gds.hessen.de/INTERSHOP/rest/WFS/HLBG-Geodaten-Site/-/downloadcenter?path=<path>[&page=N]`
    (`navigation=all` for the tree), products `3D-Daten/Digitales Geländemodell (DGM1)` and
    `3D-Daten/Digitales Oberflächenmodell (DOM1)`.
  - District and municipality names are matched on a normalised key (case, umlauts, `_`/`-`/space,
    "Kreisfreie Stadt"/"Landeshauptstadt"/"Kreis" dropped); the file name minus its product suffix is the
    municipality.
  - Files `https://gds.hessen.de` + `downloadLink.uri` (percent-encoded). The URL is never cached.
  - Metadata xlsx: URL from the DGM1 product description in the REST answer. Sheet 1, header row with
    `Kachelname` (7 digits, `EEENNNN`), `Bildflugdatum` (Excel serial), `Gemeinde`, `Kreis`.
  - Members `DGM1_32_EEE_NNNN_1_he.tif` / `DOM1_…`, matched **case-insensitively** (`dgm1_32_…` in older builds).
  - The tile cache `cache/hvbg/<model>/` is checked against the member CRC.
  - Decoded by `decode_float_tiff` (LZW + Predictor 2 Float32 must work); −9999 is NoData.
  - The record date is the `Bildflugdatum` of the centre tile (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
- **ALKIS (§8):**
  - `WfsService` `HVBG`: `https://www.gds.hessen.de/wfs2/aaa-suite/cgi-bin/alkis/vereinf/wfs`,
    `ave:GebaeudeBauwerk`, `ave:NutzungFlurstueck`, `Utm32`, `square_bez: "ohne Unterart (Hessen)"`.
  - LGB spellings, no normalisation.
- **Attribution (§9):**
  `Geobasisdaten © Hessische Verwaltung für Bodenmanagement und Geoinformation (HVBG) <year>, dl-de/zero-2-0, verändert`.

## Review Focus

1. **Range answers that are empty or short** (§2.2 saw one empty range) → retried, then a clear error; never a NaN
   terrain without a message.
2. **A tile that lies in several municipal zips, or in a zip of a neighbouring district** → found once, and
   mosaicked without seams.
3. **WMS:**
   - an oversize piece or an XML answer → an error, not a JPEG decode failure;
   - a hung request → a timeout and a retry.
4. **Negative 25833 eastings** in the region asset and in areas in western Hesse (Wiesbaden, Limburg): region
   detection, size check, estimate and the selector work.
5. **Existing regions are unchanged:**
   - the WMS pieces of GeoSN, LVermGeo and LGV stay at 4000 px;
   - the Hamburg zip reader behaves as before after its move.
