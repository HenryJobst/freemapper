# Changes

[Deutsche Version](CHANGELOG.md) · [User manual](docs/manual/en.md)

All notable changes to freemapper are listed here, newest first. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Permit notes from more states (ADR 0130): water protection areas with their zone from Berlin, Hesse and Saarland; forest functions from Brandenburg (Naturwald, Totalreservat, closed areas, Erholungswald), North Rhine-Westphalia (Naturwaldzellen, wilderness development areas) and Saarland (Naturwaldzellen, Urwald); in Saarland also the Revier of the SaarForst Landesbetrieb in the state forest and the listed buildings. freemapper describes these services only as a table of their layers; each keeps only the fields it reads. A zone the data write only as "I" or "III" is "Schutzzone I" in the notes. Bremen and Baden-Württemberg give no licence for their water protection areas in the service and stay out for now.
- Permit notes for Hamburg (ADR 0129): for a map area in Hamburg the notes name the Bezirk with its Bezirksamt, the public green spaces of the Grünplan, the monuments (garden monuments, ensembles, listed buildings) with the Denkmalschutzamt, the Revierförsterei of the Stadtwald, Bannwald and Erholungswald, and the water protection areas with their zone, all from six services of the Geoportal Hamburg. New for every state with such data: a hint for each water protection area in the area that says whether it is an inner zone (I or II), and hints on recreation forest, protected forest, natural forest and closed forest areas. freemapper takes none of the architects the Hamburg monument list names.
- Permit notes, step 3 (ADR 0124): in Brandenburg, Berlin and North Rhine-Westphalia the notes name the forest administration of the map area, each office with its Revier or district and its share: the Oberförstereien of the Landesbetrieb Forst Brandenburg, the Forstämter of the Berliner Forsten and the Regionalforstämter of Wald und Holz NRW. In North Rhine-Westphalia the share of state forest comes with them. A forest map gets the hint on the office in charge and that the forest owners must agree; in the other states only the second, with the sentence that the notes do not name the office there. In North Rhine-Westphalia a fetch downloads two files of about 17 MB together for this. freemapper takes none of the foresters' names and contact data in those files, not even into `data/permits/`.
- Forest maps in steep land get contours every 10 m instead of 5 m by themselves (ADR 0125). Steep means: on more than a tenth of the area the 5 m contours would lie closer than 0.35 mm and run into a brown area. `derive` says so in a line; `--contour-interval 5` keeps 5 m. Among the examples it is the Bode valley and the Bastei.

### Changed

- Permit notes follow the map area, not the kind of map (ADR 0127). The forest hints and the section "Forest administration" come as soon as OpenStreetMap has forest in the area, also on a sprint or schoolyard map, and also when that cannot be told without an OSM extract. A map in the middle of a town no longer gets them, even when it lies in a Revier of Brandenburg. The operator of a school in the area is now named on every kind of map, not only on schoolyard maps.
- Examples: the Bode valley and the Bastei have contours every 10 m instead of 5 m; on the steep slopes they ran together into a brown area. The rocks of the Bastei come from OSM, not from the terrain model as the examples overview said so far.

## [0.19.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added

- Bavaria (ADR 0123): maps in all of Bavaria, in UTM zone 32, also east of 12° E. The orthophoto comes from the map service of the Bayerische Vermessungsverwaltung, with the date of the flight. freemapper reads the terrain model (DGM1) tile by tile, about 2.8 MB each. Bavaria has no surface model at 1 m; freemapper takes the DOM20 of 20 cm (about 45 MB per km²), reduces it to 1 m and keeps only the reduced tiles. Bavaria has no service for its cadastre. So buildings and canopies come from the LoD2 tiles, one file per 2 km square (about 3 MB in the country, 60 to 160 MB in a city centre), and the land use from the file of each Kreis at the area (2 to 13 MB). Bridges and walls in LoD2 do not go onto the map. The estimate before the run lists all five downloads. freemapper downloads a cached file again only when the server has a newer one; offline (`--offline`) the same map is made. Stairs, walls and embankments from the cadastre and vegetation from point clouds are not there for Bavaria yet. Examples: the old town of Regensburg (sprint map) and the Schmausenbuck in the Nürnberger Reichswald (forest map). Thuringia is now the only state without data sources; the warning for areas beyond the coverage names Thuringia alone.
- Examples: a forest map (1:10 000) for every state; besides the Dammheide in Berlin, 13 new ones in `examples/`, from the Elbe sandstone at the Bastei to the Bürgerpark in Bremen. They were chosen by relief and path network out of some 30 trial runs. In Brandenburg, North Rhine-Westphalia and Saarland they hold the vegetation from laser point clouds as candidates; in Rhineland-Palatinate the state office's Metalink file does not match the files for the tiles near Dahn, so freemapper leaves them out. The table is in the examples overview.

### Changed

- Examples: the 14 sprint maps in `examples/` are rebuilt, all but Regensburg, which already comes from the current code. They now show what version 0.18.0 draws. Small compact areas are no longer dropped (ADR 0122), so the old towns have more out-of-bounds areas (520) among the candidates and more paved areas (501.5) on the map: Lübeck has 1834 candidates instead of 1365, Wismar 1660 instead of 1416. With the compact path writer the 14 training maps take 5.6 MB together instead of 10.1 MB. The tables and numbers in `examples/README.en.md` and the comparison of the three content profiles for Cochem are counted again.

### Fixed

- Saarland: in forest areas the ALKIS land use was missing with the warning "LVGL ALKIS returned 22 of 26 features in the area", also after a new `fetch`. The service lists a forest once per vegetation feature (such as deciduous wood and short-rotation coppice), with the same object id and the same geometry. freemapper keeps one row of them and now counts the others towards the service's matches. A feature repeated on a later page is still an error. At the Saarschleife near Mettlach this brings 684 land use areas and 132 more out-of-bounds candidates.

## [0.18.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added

- Baden-Württemberg (ADR 0119): maps in Baden-Württemberg, in UTM zone 32. The orthophoto comes from the map service of the LGL, with the flight date of the tile at the centre of the area. freemapper reads the terrain model (DGM1) and the surface model tile by tile out of the 2 km archives of the LGL, about 3.5 MB per tile. The surface is the nDOM1 from the aerial images of a summer flight: it shows the trees in leaf and usually comes from the flight of the orthophoto or the one before it. Buildings and land use come from the ALKIS service of the LGL. It holds whole buildings only, so no canopies, and street areas include the green beside them. OSM comes from the extracts of the four Regierungsbezirke (130 to 215 MB). Credit: `Datenquelle: LGL, www.lgl-bw.de, dl-de/by-2-0 (Daten geändert, <year>)`. Example: Heidelberg old town and castle.
- Bremen (ADR 0121): maps in Bremen and Bremerhaven, in UTM zone 32. The orthophoto comes from the map service of the Landesamt GeoInformation Bremen, which has one layer per flight and city; freemapper takes the newest colour flight of each city (2025) and gives the flight year as the date. It reads the terrain model (DGM1) and the surface model (DOM1) tile by tile out of the archive of the city, about 3.5 MB per tile. Both come from the laser scans of 2017 (Bremen) and 2015 (Bremerhaven); freemapper warns at the fetch that newer buildings and trees may be missing from the nDSM. Bremen has no simplified ALKIS. Buildings, building parts, canopies and land use therefore come from the full ALKIS, which the LGLN runs for Bremen, with the same names as in the other states. The same service gives the surveyed lines as in Brandenburg: stairs, ramps, retaining walls, monuments, embankment tops and bridges. An area in Lower Saxony that reaches into Bremen now gets buildings and land use from Bremen's cadastre there, in place of the warning that it lies outside the supported states; that warning now names Bavaria and Thuringia as examples. The area page shows the aerial photo of the city of Bremen only, not of Bremerhaven. Credit: `© GeoBasis-DE / Landesamt GeoInformation Bremen (<year>), CC BY 4.0, Daten geändert`. Example: old town of Bremen and Wallanlagen.
- Permit notes in Berlin (ADR 0120): when the map area lies in Berlin, `permits.md` and `permits.json` also name the Bezirke with their share, the green spaces with their Bezirk and dedication, the number of public playgrounds, the share of the area owned by the state of Berlin and by the Bund (one line per owner; any other owner is not named and shares the line "Other public owners"), the monuments per kind with up to 20 links into the Denkmaldatenbank, and natural monuments, protected landscape features and areas with special rules. The hints then name, for each Bezirk that is more than an edge of the area, the Ordnungsamt, the Straßen- und Grünflächenamt, the lower monument authority and the lower nature conservation authority of the Bezirksamt. The data come from five services of the Geoportal Berlin (dl-de/zero-2-0, the licence text verbatim in the table of sources). freemapper asks them only when permit notes are wanted and the area lies in Berlin, and only through the hosts of Berlin's adapter. A service that is down takes only its own section with it. A region adapter can now bring such sources (`RegionAdapter::permit_sources`). `permits.json` stays at schema 1; older files and `data/permits/sources.json` still read. Described in the user manual, chapter 12.

### Changed

- Smaller PDFs: `--pdf` and `--pdf-candidates` write their paths with the compact path writer of freemapper-core 0.26.0 (freemapper-core decision 0033). Path coordinates are rounded to 1/1000 point. Small shapes of four points or more within 7 mm, such as the dots of point symbols, are written relative to their first point, so repeated symbols compress well. The 26 PDFs of the 12 example maps with data (Dammheide left out) shrink by 22 to 79 %, from 16.9 MB to 8.3 MB together; Sanssouci's map goes from 1.45 MB to 304 KB. The maps look the same: rasterised at 150 dpi with poppler, 6 of 113 million pixels differ by more than 12 %. Texts, fonts, colours, page size and the clip to the map area stay as they were. A shape with a coordinate that is not a finite number is left out instead of breaking the page. All libraries of freemapper-core move to v0.26.0; 0.26.0 changes nothing else for freemapper.
- School and forest maps: the minimum sizes of the norm now grow with the symbols (ADR 0118). On a school map (symbols at 125 %) the smallest building or canopy is 0.625 mm instead of 0.5 mm, on a forest map at 1:10 000 (150 %) 0.75 mm; likewise the shortest line by ISSprOM 4.5, the smallest areas of out-of-bounds land, paving, green, open land and water, the shortest contour and the smallest contour ring, landforms and the smallest vegetation area. Such maps show a little less small detail. Sprint maps (100 %) stay the same. The contours, landforms and vegetation of an existing project change only when `derive` runs again. The Dammheide example is rebuilt with it. Most of what went are small buildings and canopies in the settlements and short index contours: 560 buildings instead of 757, 7 canopies instead of 24.
- Internal: lengths on paper now have a type (ADR 0118). A paper measure (`PaperMm`) holds on the printed sheet, a symbol measure (`SymbolMm`) grows with the symbol enlargement of the map kind and cannot be converted without it. The roughly 45 hand-written conversions to ground metres and map coordinates are replaced; the norm's line gap, line widths and point sizes are written down once in `build/symbol_measures.rs`. No measure changes, the maps stay the same.
- Internal: the rule for overlapping walls, fences and hedges now converts its distances like every other rule (addendum to ADR 0118). On a forest map at 1:10 000 two limits move by the last digit of the floating-point number; all other maps compute as before. The shortest stubs of a cut line stay 1 mm on the sheet and do not grow with the symbols; that is now decided. Four tests of the order of the map assembly run without a project folder (`build/assembly.rs`).

### Fixed

- Baden-Württemberg: when the server answers 404 for the 2 km archive of a block that holds a tile inside the state, the fetch of the terrain now stops with an error that names the archive's address (for the surface model a warning names it), instead of leaving a 1 km hole in the templates with only a warning about a missing tile.
- Small compact areas are no longer dropped as "too narrow" (ADR 0122). Out-of-bounds land, paving, green, open land and water leave out slivers: pieces below (1 mm)² or narrower than 1 mm. The width used to be 2 · area / perimeter, which is only half the side of a square: a square piece needed a side of 2 mm to count as 1 mm wide. Now the short side of the rectangle with the same area and perimeter counts. On forest maps 1 mm of the norm is 15 m on the ground; there, plots up to 30 × 30 m fell out of the out-of-bounds areas and left white holes in the settlements. The Dammheide example now keeps 144 of 146 out-of-bounds areas (113 without the fix) and 9 of 10 open-land areas (2 without). Sprint maps gain a few small pieces, mostly out-of-bounds candidates of 40 to 100 m²; no area that was drawn before is dropped. Strips are as wide as before, and the rule for narrow ALKIS water (a candidate below 2 m mean width) stays.
- Forest maps: the north lines are now 20 mm (1:15 000) or 30 mm (1:10 000) apart, 300 m on the ground, as ISOM 2017-2 requires; before they were 450 m apart, as in ISOM 2000. The Dammheide example is rebuilt with it.
- The OCAD hint after `--ocad`: on a forest map freemapper told you to load the ISSprOM 2019 or school symbol set in OCAD. The hint now names the set of the map kind: ISOM 2017-2 for forest maps, ISSprOM 2019 for sprint maps, the IOF school set 2019 for schoolyard maps. The same goes for the summary through MCP and on the area page. The README now names ISOM 2017-2 as well.

## [0.17.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added

- Forest map example: [Dammheide in Berlin](examples/berlin-dammheide/), 1:10 000, with the OOM map, the OCAD starting point and the candidates PDF (ADR 0116).
- Forest maps (ADR 0116): the contour interval can be set when making a map: `--contour-interval <metres>|auto` on `init`, `derive` and `run`, the MCP parameter `contour_interval` of `create_project` and `start_pipeline` (0 means automatic), and the field "Contour interval" on the area page, also for an existing project. The choice is saved in `freemapper.toml`, and the next `derive` traces the contours at it. A value that is no positive number is refused before a project folder is made.
- Permit notes (ADR 0117): `freemapper permits <dir>` and `run --permits` write `permits.md` and `permits.json` next to the map. For the map area the file names the state, Kreis, Amt and Gemeinde from the BKG's VG250, the protected areas of the BfN, further protected areas and the operators and owners from OpenStreetMap, each with its share of the area, and says whom one usually asks. It is a hint, not legal advice. The notes are made only on request; only then does freemapper ask `geodienste.bfn.de` and `sgx.geodatenzentrum.de`. It reads the OSM extract from the cache, and `--offline` takes the data stored in `data/permits/`. The area page has the option "Permit notes" for it, the MCP server the tool `permits_for_area` (a job; `project_status` carries the text as `permits_md`) and `permits` on `start_pipeline` and `open_area_selector`. `project_status` now lists the written files (`files`) of every job. The map, `freemapper.toml` and `ATTRIBUTION.txt` stay unchanged. Described in the user manual, chapter 12; the chapters after it move up by one.
- Forest maps (ADR 0116, addendum): when the contour interval is not the kind's, for example 2.5 m in a flat forest, `derive` writes it into `freemapper.toml` as `[contours] interval_m`. freemapper-print reads it there through the suite contract (freemapper-core 0.25.0) and no longer proposes 5 m. Projects at the kind's interval keep their file unchanged.
- Forest maps (ADR 0116): `--kind forest` makes a map after ISOM 2017-2 at 1:15 000 (default), 1:10 000 or 1:7500, with the symbols at 100, 150 or 200 %. It is meant for a quick training in small forests. The paths come from OSM and go straight onto the map, sorted into the path classes 502 to 507; stairs become 532. Sidewalks along roads, crossings, driveways and parking aisles are left out. The forest stays white paper: where OSM or ALKIS says forest, freemapper draws no open land, not over ALKIS parks either. The only green is OSM scrub, as a candidate. Contours lie every 5 m, every 2.5 m in flat land; `contour_interval_m` in `freemapper.toml` sets the interval. The symbol set is ISOM 2017-2 from OpenOrienteering Mapper with the colours of IOF Rev. 4. Tried on the Dammheide and the Mittelheide in Berlin. The help, the MCP server and the area page know the kind, its scales and its error messages.

### Changed

- freemapper-core 0.25.0: the suite contract knows the scales of the forest map (0.23.0) and the contour interval in `[contours] interval_m` (0.25.0), and freemapper writes both.
- Internal: placing the objects by the content settings now lives in the new module "map assembly" (`build/assembly.rs`, ADR 0115) instead of between the file reads of the project. It reads no file and can be tested with a few objects in memory. First of three steps; the maps stay byte for byte.
- Internal: resolving the duplicates between the sources (register before ALKIS before survey before OSM) and the point generalization now live in the map assembly too (`assembly::resolve`, ADR 0115, step 2). The project draws the sources and hands them over; the order of the steps is in one place.
- Internal: the map assembly now builds the whole map from the data that was read (`assembly::build`, ADR 0115, step 3). The project reads the folder in one place and makes the documents from the result; contours, landforms and vegetation are drawn in modules of their own under `build/`. A test can build a map without a project folder.
- Internal: which of Berlin's layers are registers is said in one place only, in Berlin's adapter. The rank of duplicate objects, the draw rules for trees and the credit read it from there instead of from lists of their own. Maps and credits do not change (update in ADR 0114).
- Internal: a tree cadastre is asked under the host rules of the adapter that brings it and counts for that adapter's ground. Before, the engine looked the adapter up by the id in the row of the cadastre. Had the row named another adapter of the registry, the cadastre would have been asked for that adapter's ground and under its host rules; with an unknown id it was left out. No adapter of today was affected: a manifest always writes its own id (update in ADR 0114).

### Fixed

- Forest maps (ADR 0116): impassable vegetation is ISOM 2017-2 410 instead of the first edition's 411. The path classes 502 to 505 belong to the content group `paths`, so `--content-set paths=candidates` moves every path. Stairs tagged `incline=down` run uphill as on sprint maps. A forest map no longer fetches the areas and kerbs of the Berlin street survey or the city tree cadastres, which it does not draw. The test whether an area is flat reads the terrain model every 5 m only. The margin names the interval the contours were traced at, also when `contour_interval_m` was changed since.
- Hamburg, Saarland, Saxony-Anhalt and adapters from folders: when the surface model cannot be fetched, a model stored earlier stays only if it covers the map area, and the warning names the day it is from. Before, the model of another area stayed in the project with its record, and the warning spoke of a missing nDSM template in every case, even when `derive` still made one from the old model. The other states already behaved this way.

## [0.16.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added

- Vegetation from laser point clouds in Saarland too (ADR 0105): LVGL offers the 2025 point cloud in six large zip64 archives by district, on the same share as the height models. freemapper reads only the tiles of the map area out of them by byte ranges, about 40 MB per square kilometre, and checks them against the archive's CRC. The scan was made in February without leaves, and the point cloud is thinned to one point per 0.5 m; freemapper still finds the crowns. Checked on the old town of Saarlouis. Saxony gets no point clouds for now: GeoSN took them off its download area in November 2026.
- Vegetation from laser point clouds in Rhineland-Palatinate too (ADR 0105): `fetch` loads the LAZ tiles of LVermGeo RP, mostly 110 to 500 MB per square kilometre, and keeps a tile only when its size and SHA-256 match the Metalink file of the state. The day of the scan comes from the metadata of the tile; when the tile is cached, freemapper asks only for that. Checked on the Stadtpark in Mainz.
- Vegetation from laser point clouds in Berlin too (ADR 0105): the Geoportal Berlin offers the point cloud only in nine large zip64 archives. freemapper reads just the tiles of the map area out of them by byte ranges, about 240 MB per square kilometre, checks them against the archive's CRC and keeps them in the cache as LAZ (about 100 MB). The 2021 scan was made without leaves, so freemapper recognises dense shrubs there less well. Checked on the Viktoriapark.

### Changed

- The manual's pictures of the area page are taken again (area selection, options, progress), with a new picture of the section "What goes onto the map" and all its groups. The hint of the profile "Balanced" on the page now says what the profile does: contours and the areas that shall not be entered along railway tracks go onto the map, and stairs, banks, monuments and passages also come from OpenStreetMap.
- The examples Sanssouci, Viktoriapark, Monschau, Cochem and Saarlouis are rebuilt with `--content-set vegetation=candidates`: their training maps now show the vegetation from the point clouds. The map objects of the first four are unchanged; Saarlouis has 109 street areas instead of 32 and the Saar from ALKIS, through the fix of the land uses. Bautzen stays without vegetation, because Saxony offers no point clouds at present.
- The boundaries of Lower Saxony and Schleswig-Holstein no longer overlap in the mouth of the Elbe. The cadastres of both states hold the same 89 km² of water off Cuxhaven; freemapper now splits that area at the line of equal distance from the two claim lines (ADR 0113). Maps do not change, the area is open water.
- The [adapter guide](docs/adapters/en.md) says what a code adapter can bring beyond the cadastre and the rasters: sets of extra layers and raster templates. ADR 0114 records how Berlin's registers and the K5 moved from the engine into the adapter and what stays open.
- Internal: the engine no longer asks "is this Berlin?" anywhere. The warning about missing buildings, the list of templates and every fetch for an adapter go through the registry; the K5 service lives with Berlin's adapter, the reader of stored layers in `freemapper-sources`. A test holds that the engine names no state outside the draw rules and reaches into the adapter crate only at the lines listed there. Maps, messages and files do not change.
- Internal: an adapter can name raster templates it fetches with code of its own (`RegionAdapter::templates`). Berlin's map 1:5000 (K5) is the first: the engine asks the adapter of the registry and takes the date, the messages and the item of the estimate from there. The template, the messages and the estimate do not change.
- Internal: an adapter can name sets of extra layers it fetches with code of its own (`RegionAdapter::extras`). Berlin's registers and surveys are the first such set: the engine asks the adapter of the registry instead of calling the Berlin module, and takes the item of the estimate from there too. Maps, messages, files and estimates do not change; the one exception is under "Fixed".
- Internal: the extra layers of a project (Berlin's registers, the full ALKIS, the tree cadastres) are fetched, stored, removed and read in one place instead of in three equal copies. A first step towards an engine that names no state; maps, messages and files do not change.
- Internal: the modules in `freemapper::sources` that only passed on what has lived in `freemapper-adapters-de` and `freemapper-sources` since the adapter work are removed, and so is the raster fetch for Saxony-Anhalt in program code that nothing used any more. The state still fetches through the services its manifest names; maps and requests do not change.

### Fixed

- Vegetation from point clouds stops at areas that shall not be entered (520). Before, the forest candidates punched white holes into the olive green private gardens and courtyards of the candidates map and the training PDF, because the white of the forest lies above the olive green in the colour order. On the map this holds for the areas of the map, in the candidates for those of the map and of the candidates (ADR 0105).
- `freemapper adapters check --live` for an area near Berlin no longer fetches Berlin's registers and the K5 template when another adapter is checked. Before, those requests went out without the host rules of Berlin's adapter, and the next command dropped the records again.
- Saarland: the WFS of LVGL writes the land use classes `Strassenverkehr` and `Fliessgewässer`. freemapper knew only `Straßenverkehr` and `Fließgewässer`, so Saarland maps lacked the street areas from ALKIS, the rivers from ALKIS and everything that depends on them, and the vegetation from the point cloud put open land on the streets. Every land use class from a WFS is now brought into the LGB spelling, as for Hamburg already; in Rhineland-Palatinate `Unland, Vegetationslose Fläche` thereby reads `Unland/Vegetationslose Fläche` as everywhere else.

## [0.15.0] - 2026-11-12

### Added

- New template local relief (`templates/relief.png`, layer `relief`): the terrain model minus its smoothed mean. Banks, mounds, hollows and ditches stand out light or dark; the general fall of the ground disappears. It is hidden in the map and can be switched on in OOM.
- New candidates from the terrain model (ADR 0104): small knolls (109), small elongated knolls (110), small depressions (111), earth banks (104) and, from 3 m high, impassable cliffs (201). They show what the contours of sprint and schoolyard maps miss: play mounds, sledging hills, hollows, terrace edges. A knoll has to be at least 1 m high on a sprint map and 0.5 m on a schoolyard map, and narrow enough that the contours do not show it already. The ticks of banks point down. None are made at buildings, in water or next to walls, fences and banks already drawn. All of them are candidates to be checked in the terrain (all of them belong to the new group `landforms`, banks and cliffs too; `full` or `--content-set landforms=map` puts them on the map, while `slopes=map` still takes only the banks of ALKIS and OSM); the DGM1 often misses walls narrower than a metre. Knolls and depressions do not come on residential, mixed, commercial and traffic land or in allotments, depressions not within 8 m of a building; cliffs along streets and buildings are offered as impassable walls (515), and bent steps are split into pieces that follow them. `derive` writes them to `data/landforms.geojson`. The ideas come from karttapullautin and omapmaker; the code is written anew.
- Vegetation from laser point clouds (ADR 0105), in Brandenburg and North Rhine-Westphalia: open land (401), runnable forest (405), the green shades 406, 408 and 410, and undergrowth (407, 409) as candidates. The new content group `vegetation` is off in every profile; `--content-set vegetation=candidates` (or `=map`, or the area page) makes `fetch` load the classified point clouds of LGB or Geobasis NRW for the map area into the shared cache, about 160 MB per square kilometre tile in Brandenburg and mostly 50 to 165 MB in North Rhine-Westphalia (each NRW file is checked against the size its index gives), which the download estimate and the disk check count. They are the first part left out when the disk is short, and a failing download keeps the cached tiles. `derive` writes `data/vegetation.geojson`, leaving out synthetic fill points, cellar points and bridges, the returns at buildings and the cells whose high returns are mostly single echoes (roofs, also those the cadastre no longer has); the export cuts the areas at buildings, water and paved areas and, where the map already has a park or wood of the same symbol, shows only the part that differs. Checked on Sanssouci and the Volksgarten in Cologne.

### Changed

- Internal: the tests run faster. The dependencies are fully optimized in the development build too (built once, then cached); our own crates stay unoptimized. In CI the `check` job drops from 8 to 10 minutes to 5.5 to 7, the golden net from 2 to 3 minutes to 1 to 2 per system; the outputs of the golden net stayed byte-equal. CI writes line numbers only as debug information, and a newer push to a pull request cancels its older run. A pull request runs on Linux only; with the label `all-systems`, and on every push to main, all three systems run. The first debug build after updating compiles the dependencies once more; the release build does not change.
- All twelve examples are rebuilt with the default profile `balanced`. Ten of them predated the content profiles; now contours, paths, stairs, walls and trees are on their maps, and the candidates are mostly out-of-bounds areas, control sites and earth banks. The [comparison page](examples/compare.en.md) shows one area, Cochem, in the three profiles `safe`, `balanced` and `full`, with a PDF each. The numbers in the examples overview and the manual are counted again.

## [0.6.2] - 2026-11-09

### Security

- A project folder from someone else could make freemapper write outside it through links. If `ATTRIBUTION.txt` was a link, every `run` with an existing map overwrote the file it pointed to. With `--force`, the backups `.omap.bak` and `.ocd.bak` copied the project's map into the link's target, for example `~/.zshrc`. The image copies into `ocad/` followed links too, and so did every file written into a `data`, `templates` or `ocad` folder that was a link. freemapper now writes each file under a new name and renames it into place, which replaces a link instead of writing through it. Partial files get a fresh name each time and never take over an existing file or link (ADR 0060).

A template image that was a link to a file elsewhere, such as `~/.ssh/id_rsa`, ended up copied into `ocad/`. freemapper now copies only files of the project itself. The OCAD export leaves such a template out and warns (`warning: …: a link or outside the project folder, so freemapper does not copy it; …`). freemapper checks every single write, not just once per run (ADR 0060).
- A remaining race: someone who can write to a project folder while freemapper runs on it, and who swaps a folder for a link at the right moment, can still redirect a write with a fixed name. Do not run freemapper on project folders that others can write to at the same time (ADR 0060).

### Security

- A crafted `freemapper.toml` could make freemapper delete files outside the project. A `berlin_layers` entry named like `../../../x` made `fetch` delete the file `x.geojson` next to the project folder. freemapper now builds the paths of the Berlin layers only from its own layer list. When it loads a project, it drops entries with an unknown name or with `/`, `\`, `..`, a drive letter or control characters, and warns (`warning: freemapper.toml: ignored the Berlin layer entry …`). If the folder `data/berlin` leads out of the project through a link, freemapper writes and deletes nothing there (ADR 0059).

## [0.6.0] - 2026-11-09

### Added

- Berlin: the street survey (Straßenbefahrung) of Geoportal Berlin, recorded from 2014 to 2016. On sprint maps, curbs (501.1), advertising columns (531), green strips (401) and surveyed sidewalks, squares and park paths outside the ALKIS street areas (501.5) become candidates. On both map kinds, stairs, walls, railings, traffic barriers and crash barriers (518), bus shelters and fountains are added. Each surveyed candidate carries its survey date; when that is unreadable, it says 2014-01-01 with `freemapper:currency_guessed=yes`.

- Berlin: a register point replaces OSM and surveyed points of the same kind. A lamp replaces OSM lamps up to 5 m and surveyed masts up to 2 m away, a fountain other fountains up to 3 m away. On schoolyard maps a surveyed hydrant, bench, mast or play item replaces the OSM one up to 2 m away. A fountain of a register or of the street survey inside drawn water is dropped.
- Berlin: buildings carry their footprint date (Grundrissaktualität) from the building heights of 2022 in the Umweltatlas as data currency. A building without a match keeps no date.
- New attribution lines for the street survey, the registers and the building heights, each only when they gave objects. The register line names only the registers whose points are in the map or the candidates.

### Changed

- Berlin: the paved areas (501.5) no longer include the surveyed green strips; freemapper cuts them out. Tree pits stay inside. A green strip is a 401 candidate only where freemapper cut it out or where it lies off the paved areas. A cut area names the strips in `freemapper:cut`, `freemapper:cut_ids` and `freemapper:cut_currency`. An existing map changes only with `run --force`.
- Berlin: short line pieces from ALKIS and the street survey that meet end to end are joined into one line. It names the other pieces in `freemapper:also` and carries the oldest survey date. Lines under 1 mm on paper are dropped after that, now for ALKIS too.
- Berlin: when an object comes from several sources, the register wins over ALKIS, ALKIS over the street survey, and the survey over OSM. Each Berlin candidate carries the tag `berlin:layer`.
- The download estimate counts 10.5 instead of 4 MB per km² for the Berlin layers (measured at Viktoriapark: 9.8).

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-09

### Added

- Disk space: before each download freemapper checks the free space of the cache and the project folder, each drive on its own, and keeps 1 GB free on every drive. If not everything fits, it leaves out the surface model, the update of an older OSM extract and, in Berlin, the structures, trees and K5 and warns with the space needed and the space free. Without room for the map data, the terrain model or the orthophoto it stops before it downloads anything. When the disk fills up during a write, freemapper says so plainly, deletes the half-written file and does not retry. The download estimate (MCP tool `estimate_download`, area selection page) shows the free space and marks what does not fit.
- Berlin: more open data from Geoportal Berlin. ALKIS canopies and carports become map objects (522) on both map kinds. freemapper cuts them at buildings and leaves them out when they lie almost wholly inside a building. Candidates now include stairs (532.6 to 532.9) with the walking direction from the terrain model. A stair that does not rise there leads up to an adjoining building or monument, or runs across its short side. Stairs wider than three times 532.9 become parallel lines. The `Freitreppe` lines are step edges, and each flight of parallel edges becomes one stair across them. Further candidates are walls, retaining walls, fences, hedges, building passages and monuments, and on sprint maps also bridges and embankment tops. Street and park trees from the tree cadastre become 417 or 418 by crown size, and OSM trees within 2 m of them are dropped. So are OSM fences, walls and hedges with 80 % of their length within 1.5 m of the same ALKIS line. The Berlin map 1:5000 (K5) comes as the template `templates/k5.png`. An existing map gets the canopies only with `run --force`, and `run` says so.
- `init --osm` takes one part of an OSM area of several separate parts with `--part <n>` (1 is the largest; `part` over MCP). Without `--part` the message lists the parts with their sizes. When Nominatim does not find a way, such as an untagged outer way, the message suggests the relation or `--geojson`.

### Changed

- Allotment gardens (ALKIS land use "Sport-, Freizeit- und Erholungsfläche" with `bez` "Kleingarten") are out-of-bounds candidates (520) in every state. Parks, sports grounds and playgrounds stay open.

### Fixed

- Contour candidates name the terrain model of their state in `freemapper:source`, e.g. `laiv-mv-dgm1` or `hvbg-dgm1`. Before, every state said `lgb-dgm1`.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-08

### Added

- Lower Saxony (LGLN): orthophoto from the WMS, terrain and surface models (DGM1/DOM1) from the WCS in 2 km pieces, flight date and data currency from the STAC searches, buildings and land use from ALKIS. The nDSM is DOM1 − DGM1; if the DOM1 is more than two years older than the orthophoto, freemapper warns with both dates. A message says whether the trees were in leaf on the flight. Maps use UTM zone 32. Bremen is not included yet.
- Rhineland-Palatinate (LVermGeo RP): orthophoto from the WMS, with the block's date of creation as the flight date. Terrain and surface models (DGM1/DOM1) come from 1 km laser tiles whose names and dates the `Laserbefliegung` flight index gives; for tiles without a flight year freemapper reads the state's Metalink file. If the server lacks a tile, freemapper reports it as missing and asks again after 30 days. Buildings and land use come from ALKIS. Maps use UTM zone 32. At the Hesse border (Mainz, Wiesbaden) freemapper reads the ALKIS and OSM of both states.
- Water on all maps: rivers, canals, lakes, ponds, harbour basins and sea from ALKIS become uncrossable water (301.1) with a bank line (301.6), with no lines between cadastre areas or along the map edge. Water with a mean width under 2 m comes as candidates. OSM adds ponds, swimming pools, fountain basins, streams, ditches, fountains, wells, springs, drinking fountains on schoolyard maps and bridges on sprint maps as candidates. Contours stop at the shore, and fountain points in water (mostly jets in a basin) are left out. In OCAD the fill and the bank line have the numbers 301.001 and 301.002. An existing map gets the water only with `run --force`.

### Changed

- The north lines are in the map part "default part" with the other map objects, tagged `freemapper:source=north-lines`. In OOM they can be broken at small objects, and on the training map walls and fences lie on top of them. Title, scale and attribution stay in the part "peripheral information". An existing map changes only with `run --force`.
- Colours follow the IOF Printing and Colour Definitions Rev. 4 (September 2024, valid from 1 December 2024) instead of the older values in OOM's symbol set: brown is 25/75/100/0 instead of 0/56/100/18, green 80/0/100/0 instead of 76/0/91/0, brown 50% 10/35/50/0. Buildings are filled with 50 % black instead of 60 %, as in ISSprOM 2019-2. This applies to the OOM map, the OCAD file and the training PDF. Colour names and order stay the same.
- Longer messages use plainer wording.
- If a map area reaches beyond every supported state (abroad, out to sea or into a state freemapper does not support yet, such as Bremen or Thuringia), freemapper warns at fetch that the orthophoto and ALKIS can be missing there. This applies to all states.

### Fixed

- Where the water areas of two states did not quite meet at the border in mid-river (Mainz, Rhine), a bank line ran through the river. freemapper now closes gaps under 0.5 m between water areas before it draws the bank lines. This also applies to parcels of one state. Bridges and roads (0.8 m and wider) stay land, and islands keep their bank lines.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-07

### Added

- First release. freemapper builds an OpenOrienteering Mapper starter project for orienteering from official geodata of Brandenburg and OpenStreetMap, as a command line tool (`init`, `fetch`, `derive`, `export`, `run`) and as an MCP server for AI assistants.
- Georeferenced map with the IOF symbol set for 1:1000 sprint maps, peripheral information (north lines, scale bar, info block) and a credit line that names freemapper and its repository.
- Orthophoto, hillshade and slope as templates behind the map, and contour lines and the nDSM derived from the terrain data.
- Buildings from ALKIS as map objects.
- OSM data as candidates: reliable OSM ways become map objects, the rest stays as candidates for you to review. Geofabrik extracts are cached, and freemapper falls back to the cached extract when Geofabrik is unreachable.
- Sprint map objects: paved areas from ALKIS traffic areas and unpaved paths from OSM.
- Out-of-bounds areas for private land, derived from ALKIS land use.
- OCAD export (`--ocad`, OCAD 12) with placeholder symbols.
- Training map as a one-page PDF (`export --pdf`, `run --pdf`, MCP option `pdf`).
- Area selection in the browser (`freemapper select`, MCP tool `open_area_selector`) with place search, rectangle, polygon, tile outlines and a download estimate.
- Place search through Nominatim (`init --place`) and initialisation from OSM (`init --osm`). The MCP server also lists layers and delivers PNG previews.
- Release archives for Linux (x86_64 and ARM64), macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows, each with a SHA-256 checksum.

### Fixed

- Downloads have connect, response and body timeouts, keep a cached copy when the new download is invalid, and clean up old partial files.
- An existing map is kept when you run again. freemapper refreshes its templates and says so.
- freemapper protects edited maps from being overwritten on export.
- Error messages name the product and what to do next. Scale 0 and out-of-range coordinates are rejected.
- Download errors for uncovered tiles come in one report, and offline runs fall back to cached data.
